Privacy

Spike AI works offline. It reads the screen of the display that shows VALORANT only to recognize the Spike icon, and processes every frame in memory on this PC. Screen images are not saved, uploaded or shared. The Valorant Console page reads VALORANT's own log file on this PC. Settings, imported voices and the diagnostics log stay on this PC.
